03 — The Knowledge Model

Three states.
Every answer classified.

Nurai does not treat all answers as equal. Every response is classified into one of three transparent states — so users always know what they are getting, and managers always know what is missing.

01
Retrieved

Direct match from corpus

The answer was found verbatim or near-verbatim in the indexed knowledge base. The response includes the exact source document and section reference, making it fully auditable.

Source linked — no interpretation added. Confidence above retrieval threshold.

02
Synthesised

Reasoned across multiple sources

The answer draws on multiple chunks from different documents. The model connects them explicitly, not by assumption. All contributing sources are listed.

Multi-source references listed. Reasoning is transparent and traceable.

03
Knowledge Gap

Not in corpus — logged, not invented

No relevant source was found above the confidence threshold. The user receives a transparent "not in our current knowledge base" response. The query is logged to the Gap Queue for content owners to act on.

Query logged to Gap Queue. Ops team notified. Corpus can be updated.

04 — Process

How Nurai works

From document upload to cited answer — a transparent, auditable pipeline with no black-box steps.

01
Connect your knowledge sources
Link Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or upload files directly. Nurai ingests PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, and web pages. Your corpus is version-controlled and auditable.
Supported: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, HTML, Notion, Confluence, email threads. Incremental sync every hour by default.
02
Deploy your Advisor to a channel
Each Advisor is configured with a persona, tone, language, and fallback behaviour. Deploy to WhatsApp, LINE, web embed, Slack, Teams, or API — or all simultaneously.
Modes: Internal staff advisor, external customer support, or hybrid. Channel permissions and audience scoping are set per Advisor.
03
Every query routes through the three-state model
RAG retrieval runs against your indexed corpus. Confidence scoring determines the response state. Retrieved, Synthesised, or Knowledge Gap — every answer is classified and logged.
Confidence threshold: Tunable per Advisor. Knowledge Gaps are never fabricated — they are logged and surfaced to your content team.

05 — Capabilities

Built for institutional
deployment at scale

Nurai is not a generic chatbot. It is a governed knowledge infrastructure designed for organisations where accuracy, auditability, and access control are non-negotiable.

Cited retrieval — always

Every Retrieved or Synthesised answer includes the exact source document, section, and update date. Compliance teams can audit any conversation end-to-end.

Role-based access control

Each Advisor can be scoped to internal staff, external users, or specific departments. Sensitive documents are restricted at the corpus level, not the prompt level.

Gap Queue and corpus analytics

Unanswerable questions are automatically logged with frequency counts. Content owners see exactly what to document next, closing the knowledge loop over time.

Multi-language support

Advisors can be configured per language. Thai, English, and regional variants supported. Automatic language detection routes queries without user configuration.
