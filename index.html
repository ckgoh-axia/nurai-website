The Problem

A wrong answer at 2 AM
is never just a mistake.

In hospitality, education, and agritech — the cost of wrong information isn't embarrassment. It's a guest walking out, a student missing a deadline, or a compliance breach nobody caught.

Hospitality
A front desk team member quoted the wrong cancellation policy. The guest posted. It cost us the booking and the review.

When policy changes don't reach every staff member, staff improvise. Inconsistency is the rule — especially across shifts and properties.

Education
Students were getting different answers about scholarship deadlines from different offices. Three missed their applications.

Student services teams field hundreds of questions a day. When the answer lives in a PDF from six months ago, nobody can find it fast enough.

Agritech
Our field agents knew the product — but not the updated application protocols. The ones from last quarter.

Distributed teams in low-connectivity environments need instant, cited answers. Not a search bar that returns five documents.

Healthcare
We had the protocol. It was documented. But the night shift team couldn't locate it fast enough to matter.

The right answer already exists — in a procedure manual, a policy update from March. The gap is retrieval, not knowledge.

The problem is not that staff don't know the answer.
It's that your organisation does — buried in documents nobody can reach at the moment that matters.

How Nurai Answers

Every answer knows
what it's based on.

Nurai classifies every response into one of three states — so you always know what you can trust, what needs checking, and what your corpus is missing.

Retrieved

Directly from your corpus

The answer exists verbatim in a connected document. Nurai returns it with a citation — document name, section, and date last synced.

↗ Cited to exact source. High confidence. Safe to act on.
Synthesised

Inferred across multiple sources

No single document has the full answer, but multiple sources together do. Nurai reasons across them and cites each one used.

→ Multi-source. Cited. Confidence level flagged.
Knowledge Gap

Not in your corpus — flagged

The corpus doesn't contain this information. Instead of guessing, Nurai says so — and logs it as a gap for your team to fill.

⚠ Honest refusal. Gap logged. Never hallucinated into corpus.
